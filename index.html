        Digital Health Risk Index (DHRI) (Phase 10)

        Not available yet
        Episodes this session-

        DHRI combines fatigue (40%), posture (25%), attention (20%) and continuous exposure without a break (15%) into one number from 0 to 100. Below 35 is LOW RISK, 35-65 is MODERATE, 65 and above is HIGH. If an input is not available yet (for example, not calibrated), DHRI is calculated from whichever inputs ARE available. DHRI is a project-specific digital wellbeing indicator, not a medically validated health score and not a diagnosis. The weights and thresholds are our own starting values and have not been validated on real users.
